Online Red Flags

  • Requests for personal information or passwords
  • Sudden pressure to keep online chats secret
  • Fake profiles or suspicious direct messages
  • Hostile comments or targeted online bullying

Deep Insight: Trust your instincts. If something online feels off or manipulative, it is a warning sign to pause and disengage.

Emotional Signals

  • Sudden anxiety or dread when checking notifications
  • Feeling drained or overwhelmed by online drama
  • Comparing yourself constantly to online feeds
  • The urge to isolate or withdraw after scrolling

Deep Insight: Emotional exhaustion from screens is a clear warning that it's time to log off and step back into reality.

What To Do

  • Block, mute, or restrict harmful accounts
  • Take regular breaks away from screens
  • Remember online drama does not define you
  • Protect your peace by setting digital boundaries

Deep Insight: You control your digital space. Using blocking tools and stepping away interrupts negativity loops.

Support Matters

  • Talk to a trusted friend, parent, or mentor
  • Report abusive content or cyberbullying safely
  • Remind yourself you never deal with it alone
  • Reaching out for help is a sign of true strength

Deep Insight: Sharing difficult online experiences with a safe adult lifts the weight and ensures proper support.
